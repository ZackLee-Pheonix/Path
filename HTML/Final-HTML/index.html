About Us

NVIDIA Corporation is a global technology company headquartered in Santa Clara, California, founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. It is best known for designing and manufacturing graphics processing units (GPUs) that are widely used in gaming, professional visualization, data centers, and artificial intelligence.
